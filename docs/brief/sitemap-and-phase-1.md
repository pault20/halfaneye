Nov 6, 2026 · @Paul Thewlis

Phase 1 launches a complete short book: a preface, eight chapters that tell the whole story end to end, a 15-entry Atlas of Eyes, five rebuttal pages, and the reference apparatus (bibliography, glossary, changelog). Everything after launch adds depth without changing the structure.

## Book conventions

The site reads like a book: a fixed table of contents, numbered chapters read in order, and a reference section you dip into.

- **Parts and chapters.** Four parts, numbered chapters (1, 2, 3…) and numbered sections (2.1, 2.2). Numbers stay fixed once published so citations never break; new material goes in new sections or appendices.
- **Linear reading.** Every chapter has previous/next links, a progress marker and a persistent table of contents sidebar.
- **Front and back matter.** Preface, how to use this book, and how it was written up front; atlas, rebuttals, glossary, bibliography and changelog at the back.
- **Science stays neutral.** Chapters explain the evidence without arguing with creationists. Rebuttals live in their own part and link back to the chapters that carry the evidence.
- **Every claim cited.** Numbered footnotes on every factual sentence, each resolving to one bibliography entry with DOI or page number.
- **Figures numbered.** "Figure 4.2" style, each with a caption, a credit line and the source it illustrates.
- **Versioned.** Each page shows "first published" and "last revised" dates, and every change is logged in the changelog. Readers can cite a page with a ready-made citation.
- **Shareable pages.** Each Atlas entry and rebuttal stands alone so a counter-apologist can drop one link into a debate.

## Later phases

Each later phase adds whole chapters or entries; nothing published in Phase 1 is renumbered.

1. **Phase 2 — Diversity.** Chapters 3 and 8–12 (physics of seeing, and all of Part III); Atlas to all 38 catalogued eyes; three more claim-and-response pages; interactive timeline; researcher video embeds per chapter.
2. **Phase 3 — Completion.** Chapters 15–16 (losing eyes, human eye failures); further reading page; Atlas entries beyond the current catalogue.
3. **Ongoing — Revision.** New papers checked into the relevant chapter, each change logged in the changelog with the page's "last revised" date updated.
